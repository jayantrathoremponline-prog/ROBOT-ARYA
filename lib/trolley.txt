Normally, as we go to any supermarket or mall then we see a lot of rush and a long queue at the bill counters. So this is one of the projects in which customer can directly see the product details and costs in which with the help of RFID cards and tags we can scan the product and get the information. In this project an RFID tag is placed at each of the product of the supermarket which has unique IDs. If we scan the product on the RFID module it will pas the information to the LCD screen attached to the trolley. Conclusion is we don't have to wait in a long queue if this project can be advanced then some features like paying bill directly can also limit the queue issues,if customer has scanned the product and don't want to buy it then with the help of a switch they can erase that product's details.
