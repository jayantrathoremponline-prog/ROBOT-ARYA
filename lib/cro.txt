A cathode ray oscilloscope (C.R.O.) is a type of electronic test instrument that uses a cathode ray tube (CRT) to display electrical signals. The CRT works by emitting a beam of electrons that is directed at a phosphor-coated screen, creating a visible pattern that represents the input signal. The CRO typically includes a variety of controls for adjusting the display, such as time and voltage scales, as well as inputs for connecting the device to the signal being measured.
